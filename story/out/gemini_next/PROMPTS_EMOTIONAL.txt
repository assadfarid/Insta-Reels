THE 10 MOST EMOTIONAL SCENES (Gemini, 16:9, name each video after its picture)
Every prompt ends with: Keep the exact hand-drawn watercolour storybook style and the same characters. Slow, gentle, subtle motion. Static camera or very slow push-in. No text, no new characters, no scene change.

s04 - The lie
The robot slowly turns its head toward the framed photograph of Mia and her mother and stays there, still; its cyan eyes dim slightly with guilt; Mia watches it quietly with hopeful eyes; red emergency light flickers softly.

s21 - Mother's recording
On the flickering blue hologram, the mother speaks with tearful eyes and slowly raises her hand toward the screen; the hologram glitches softly; the girl and the robot stand still in silhouette, watching.

s38 - "Stop lying!"
The robot tries to push itself up from the broken bridge but its arms tremble and it sinks back down; the red light on its chest blinks; Mia kneels beside it, tears in her eyes; warm sunset light.

s42 - The sacrifice
The robot picks up a heavy power cable and slowly plugs it into its own chest; small sparks; its cyan eyes flicker; Mia watches nervously; the old lamp swings slightly.

s45 - "Promise me you'll be okay"
Mia holds the robot's metal hand tightly and looks up at it with tears of hope in her eyes; she squeezes its hand; the robot's cyan eyes flicker weakly; blue console lights glow.

s46 - "I promise"
The robot slowly looks down at the crayon family drawing on its chest; its cyan eyes soften and flicker weakly; Mia gently holds its hand; quiet, tender moment.

s48 - The hug and the rescue lights
Mia hugs the fallen robot tightly, her shoulders trembling as she cries; the robot stays still and dark; the headlights of the rescue vehicle outside slowly grow brighter and sweep light through the window.

s52 - "We can start again"
Mia slowly hands the worn crayon drawing to the robot with a sad, gentle smile; the robot takes it carefully with both hands; warm morning sunbeams in the workshop.

s53 - "Mia?"
The robot looks down at the crayon drawing in its hands; its cyan eyes flicker and slowly brighten with recognition; warm sunbeams and floating dust.

s54 - "You prefer your toast unburned"
The robot's cyan eyes curve into a warm smile as it holds up the crayon drawing; Mia, in the foreground, starts to smile with tears in her eyes; warm golden sunlight.
