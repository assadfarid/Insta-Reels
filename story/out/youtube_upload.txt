Every night for five years, a little robot named ECHO-07 told eight-year-old Mia the same thing: "Your mother is coming home tomorrow."

It was a lie. 🌙

Deep underground, in a shelter lit by red emergency lights, Echo has kept one secret to protect the girl he loves. But when Mia finds a hidden recording, the truth changes everything – and the two of them must step into the world outside for the very first time.

A heartwarming, hand-painted watercolour story about love, honesty, hope, and what it really means to be a family.
