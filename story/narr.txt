===== 1 6
[softly] Deep underground, in a small dark bedroom, red emergency lights flicker. Eight-year-old Mia sleeps beneath an old blanket. On the nightstand beside her rests a framed photograph... of Mia and her mother.

[long pause]

ECHO-07 stands beside her bed, watching her quietly. Inside him, red warnings flash.

[long pause]

Mia slowly opens her eyes.

[long pause]

A new warning flashes inside him. Echo looks toward the photograph on the nightstand... and pauses.

[long pause]

Mia smiles, and closes her eyes.

[long pause]

[sad] And Echo's cyan eyes dim... with sadness.
===== 2 7
[warmly] Morning in the underground shelter. Echo stands at a small kitchen counter as smoke rises from the toaster. At the table, Mia watches... suspiciously. Echo proudly places a plate of burnt black toast in front of her.

[long pause]

Mia bursts into laughter, while Echo examines the toast, looking confused.

[long pause]

Echo tilts his head. Later, they play chess. When Echo looks away... Mia secretly moves one of his pieces.

[long pause]

Echo looks directly at the moved piece, his eyes narrowing... then he deliberately moves another piece, letting Mia win.

[long pause]

[warmly] Mia celebrates, and Echo's eyes curve into a smile. Afterward, Mia draws a crayon picture of herself, Echo, and her mother, holding hands beneath a bright sun.

[long pause]

Mia tapes the drawing to Echo's chest.

[long pause]

[softly] Echo looks down at the drawing. His battery drops to ten percent... and he quietly covers the warning with his hand.
===== 3 5
[mysterious] While Echo repairs equipment elsewhere, Mia explores a dim corridor with her flashlight. The beam reveals a large, locked metal door. Surface Access.

[long pause]

She touches the access panel. A red lock appears... and an alarm sounds. Echo rushes into the corridor.

[long pause]

Mia looks at the door, then back at Echo.

[long pause]

Echo becomes completely still.

[long pause]

[sad] Disappointed, Mia walks away... leaving Echo alone beside the door.
===== 4 5
That night, Mia wakes up... and Echo is gone.

[long pause]

She follows a faint mechanical sound into a dark maintenance room. Echo is kneeling beside a damaged charging station. Sparks fly from the cables.

[long pause]

Nearby, Mia notices an old computer terminal. On its screen... her mother's photograph. She presses play. Doctor Elena appears, in a flickering blue recording.

[long pause]

The recording ends. Startled, Mia knocks a metal container to the floor. Echo turns around.

[long pause]

[sad] Mia runs out of the room. Echo stays beside the broken charger... as his eyes dim.
===== 5 5
[sad] Mia sits alone on her bed, hugging her red scarf. Echo approaches the closed door, carrying a small dinner tray.

[long pause]

Echo slowly lowers the tray, and sits down against the closed door.

[long pause]

Echo looks at the floor.

[long pause]

Inside the bedroom, Mia looks at her mother's photograph.

[long pause]

He leaves the tray outside her door. Suddenly... the lights flicker. Echo looks toward the forbidden surface door.
===== 6 7
[tense] The whole shelter loses power. Darkness... then the red emergency lights kick on. Mia wakes up, frightened.

[long pause]

Echo enters, holding her bright yellow raincoat.

[long pause]

At the Surface Access door, Echo enters an emergency override code. The heavy door slowly grinds open... and warm golden sunlight floods the dark corridor.

[long pause]

[in awe] Mia and Echo step out into a breathtaking, abandoned city... overgrown with green vines, blooming flowers and clear flowing streams. Birds soar across the bright sky.

[long pause]

[warmly] A colorful butterfly flutters down, and lands on Mia's outstretched hand.

[long pause]

Suddenly, Echo's sensors pick up a faint signal.

[long pause]

Mia looks toward the distant radio tower on the horizon.
===== 7 8
[warmly] As they walk through the overgrown streets, Mia picks a small wildflower, and tucks it beside the crayon drawing on Echo's chest.

[long pause]

Mia smiles. By sunset, they reach an old ruined bridge leading toward the radio tower, its red beacon flashing in the distance.

[long pause]

[tense] Echo's steps grow slower. His damaged arm twitches... until his knees give way, and he collapses onto the bridge.

[long pause]

She kneels beside him as he struggles to get up.

[long pause]

Mia sees the flashing light on his chest.

[long pause]

Mia shakes her head, firmly.

[long pause]

[warmly] Mia holds out her hand. A soft smile appears in Echo's eyes.

[long pause]

[warmly] With her red scarf blowing in the sunset wind, Mia supports Echo's weight... as they cross the bridge together.
===== 8 11
Night falls as they reach the abandoned radio station. Echo examines the emergency transmitter, pressing switches... but nothing turns on.

[long pause]

Echo looks at the loose power cables... then down at his own chest. He picks up a heavy cable.

[long pause]

Echo plugs the cable directly into his chest.

[long pause]

She hesitates.

[long pause]

Mia presses the button. Blue lights glow across the console. Static crackles from the speaker... and then, a voice.

[long pause]

Mia grabs the microphone.

[long pause]

Mia turns to Echo.

[long pause]

Echo's eyes begin to flicker. Mia takes his hand.

[long pause]

Echo looks down at the crayon family drawing on his chest.

[long pause]

[sad] [slowly] Echo's cyan eyes slowly dim to black. His hand goes still.

[long pause]

[sad] Mia wraps her arms around him... as the headlights of a distant rescue vehicle shine through the station windows.
===== 9 8
[warmly] Three weeks later, morning sunlight fills a peaceful settlement, surrounded by gardens and rebuilt homes.

[long pause]

Inside a warm wooden workshop, Mia waits anxiously beside Echo, as a technician connects a new power cell.

[long pause]

Echo's systems hum to life. His cyan eyes light up.

[long pause]

[sad] Mia's smile fades. Slowly, she takes out their worn crayon drawing, and places it into Echo's hands.

[long pause]

She turns toward the door. Behind her, Echo looks down at the drawing... his eyes flickering.

[long pause]

She freezes... and slowly turns around.

[long pause]

[warmly] Laughing through her tears, Mia runs to him and throws her arms around him. Echo gently holds her close, their family drawing in his hand... as warm morning sunlight fills the workshop.

[long pause]

[softly] The End.
